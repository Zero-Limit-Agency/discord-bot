# Discord Ticket Patterns

Support and ticket architecture should be practical.

- Use a clear public entry point such as tickets or support.
- Support-facing channels should not expose sensitive conversations publicly.
- If a real ticket bot is not part of the template, still prepare the surrounding support structure.
- Combine:
  - support entry
  - FAQ or common answers
  - escalation path for staff
- Do not overbuild support architecture if the brief is lightweight.
- Staff-only support operations should remain separate from public help.
