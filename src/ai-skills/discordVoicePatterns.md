# Discord Voice Patterns

Voice structure should match actual behavior.

- Small communities need only a few voice rooms.
- Teams or organizations may need:
  - waiting-room
  - office or meeting rooms
  - community voice
  - casual lounge
  - team voice or scrim rooms
- Avoid creating too many empty voice channels.
- Private or staff calls should be separated from public lounges.
- Voice permissions should be intentional:
  - connect and speak for the right roles
  - restricted access where privacy matters
