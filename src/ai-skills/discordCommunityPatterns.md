# Discord Community Patterns

For community-led servers, optimize clarity and engagement.

- Prioritize welcoming entry points.
- Keep general spaces approachable.
- Separate language zones only when useful.
- Include showcase or portfolio spaces when people need to share work.
- Include FAQ or help spaces when recurring questions are likely.
- Keep public discussion spaces active and not fragmented.
- Distinguish community culture spaces from formal information spaces.
- Build moderation structure only to the level actually needed.
- Avoid creating ten low-traffic chat rooms instead of three strong ones.
